# Magistrate delivery manifest

## Start here

- `00_Brand_Guide/Magistrate_Brand_Guide.pdf` — eleven-page identity, interaction, and implementation guide.
- `README.md` — concise brand and product rules.
- `06_Implementation/component-behavior.md` — implementation contract derived from the Magi Bible and approved product decisions.

## Production identity

- `01_Logos/SVG` — master monochrome, light, dark, active, and lockup vectors.
- `01_Logos/PNG` — transparent raster exports at common delivery sizes.
- `02_App_Icons` — iOS, Android adaptive, and web icon assets.
- `03_Color_and_Type` — palette, typography specification, and font licensing notes.

## Product and campaign design

- `04_UI_States` — brand foundations, conversational chat, operational drawer, fleet, authenticated connections, both voice modes, and light mode.
- `05_Marketing` — organic active-state artwork in master, 16:9, square, and portrait crops.
- `05_Marketing/Backgrounds` — seven built-in atmospheric themes in editable SVG and production PNG.

## Engineering handoff

- `06_Implementation/tokens.json` — platform-neutral design tokens.
- `06_Implementation/tokens.css` — web-ready custom properties.
- `06_Implementation/motion.css` — functional and immersive active-state motion plus reduced-motion behavior.
- `06_Implementation/CHANGELOG.md` — exact page, behavior, token, and preservation record for this refinement.
- `07_Source` — editable SVG source boards and supplied visual references.
